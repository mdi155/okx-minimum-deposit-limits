## OKX crypto minimum deposit

Crypto deposits are transfers from another exchange, broker, wallet, or blockchain address into your OKX account.

The minimum is displayed when you select:

- The cryptocurrency
- The deposit network
- The account destination, if OKX offers more than one option
- Any required tag or memo

OKX states that deposits below the displayed minimum may not be credited. The platform also notes that some below-minimum deposits may only be recoverable for certain cryptocurrencies, and recovery may involve an additional fee.

That means the minimum is not just a suggestion. It is a practical requirement for the deposit to appear in your balance.

### Why does OKX have crypto minimums?

Blockchain transactions involve network processing, address aggregation, confirmations, and operational costs. A very small deposit may not be economically useful to process, particularly when the amount is too small relative to network or transfer costs.

OKX explains that minimum deposit amounts help manage transaction efficiency and ensure that deposits can be properly aggregated and credited.

The exact reason can vary by asset and network, but the result is the same: always use the current minimum shown on the deposit screen.

### Can I deposit less than the minimum?

You can technically send an amount below the displayed minimum from another platform, but that does not mean OKX will credit it.

OKX specifically warns that assets may not be received when the deposited amount is below the required minimum. In some cases, support may be able to investigate recovery, but this is not guaranteed and may involve a recovery fee.

A small test transaction can be sensible when you are unsure about a wallet address or network. However, the test amount should still meet the minimum shown by OKX. Sending a tiny amount below the limit defeats the purpose of the test.

## OKX fiat minimum deposit

Fiat deposits work differently from crypto deposits.

OKX does not show one global minimum for all bank transfers or payment methods. Instead, the available methods and limits depend on your region and account. After you select a cash deposit method, OKX shows the applicable minimum, maximum, fees, and processing time.

Cash deposit limits can include:

- Minimum amount per transaction
- Maximum amount per transaction
- Daily deposit limit
- Weekly deposit limit
- Monthly deposit limit
- Maximum number of deposits in a defined period

OKX explains that these limits can depend on the selected deposit method and verification level. Some limits reset on a rolling basis rather than at midnight. For example, a daily limit may reset 24 hours after a transaction instead of at the beginning of the next calendar day.

This is why a bank transfer minimum listed in an older article may not match the amount displayed in your own account.

### Are fiat deposits free?

Not necessarily.

The deposit preview shows whether a processing fee applies. OKX explains that fees can be flat, percentage-based, or a combination of both. When a fee applies, the amount credited to your OKX account may be lower than the amount sent.

For example, if a payment method charges a fixed fee, sending a small amount can be inefficient because the fee takes up a larger percentage of the transfer. A method with no OKX fee may still involve charges from your bank or payment provider.

Check the final preview before confirming the transaction.

- **OKX deposit fee:** May be zero for crypto deposits.

A zero deposit fee does not mean the entire transfer is cost-free. Review the sending platform’s withdrawal preview before confirming.

## Common reasons an OKX deposit is not credited

A deposit can fail to appear for several reasons:

1. The amount was below the displayed minimum.
2. The wrong network was selected.
3. The token is not supported.
4. A memo or tag was missing.
5. The blockchain has not completed enough confirmations.
6. The deposit was sent to an outdated or incorrect address.
7. The account has not completed the required verification.
8. The deposit was sent through a method that is unavailable in the user’s region.

OKX advises users to check the asset, network, confirmation status, and deposit details. Crypto deposits may not appear immediately because blockchain confirmations can take time.

If the deposit is missing, do not send another transaction immediately. First check the transaction hash, selected network, destination address, memo or tag, and whether the transaction is marked as completed by the sending platform.
